================================================================================
  SHAP EXPLAINABILITY
================================================================================

  Two independent SHAP explainers — one per stage.
  Each returns top-10 features by absolute SHAP impact for every prediction.

  Stage 1 SHAP (shap_explainer_stage1.pkl):
    Explains what drives P(cure) up or down for each account.
    Positive SHAP → increases cure probability (reduces LGD).
    Negative SHAP → decreases cure probability (increases LGD).

  Stage 2 SHAP (shap_explainer_stage2.pkl):
    Explains what drives severity E[LGD | non-cure] for each account.
    Positive SHAP → increases severity (higher LGD).
    Negative SHAP → decreases severity (lower LGD).

  Output per feature:
    feature  : column name from feature_cols
    impact   : SHAP value (float, 6 decimal places)
