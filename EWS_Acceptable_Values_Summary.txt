================================================================================
 MODEL METADATA
================================================================================

  Model Name    : EWS Personal Loan Risk Scorer
  Algorithm     : Gradient Boosting
  Training Data : 11,200 loan account records
  Accuracy      : 88%
  Summary       : Identifies currently-performing personal loan borrowers at risk
                  of missing payments within the next 6 months.

  Output Risk Tiers:
    🟢 GREEN  — Low risk. Stable repayment behaviour. No immediate action needed.
    🟡 AMBER  — Medium risk. Early stress signals detected. Proactive monitoring.
    🔴 RED    — High risk. Multiple stress signals present. Priority intervention.

================================================================================
 SENTINEL VALUE CONVENTION
================================================================================

  -1 is used as a "not applicable" sentinel (not a missing value) for columns
  that require a lookback window longer than the account's history. Rules:

    months_on_book < 12  → bounce_count_12m, late_payment_trend_6m_vs_12m,
                           n_nach_mandate_changes_12m, payment_regularity_12m,
                           n_months_salary_credited_12m, n_disputes_raised_12m,
                           mobile_change_flag_12m

    oldest_tradeline_months < 12  → cibil_change_12m, cibil_volatility_12m

    salary_account_flag = 0  → salary_credited_last_month_flag,
                               n_months_salary_credited_6m,
                               salary_credit_trend_6m_vs_12m,
                               avg_salary_credit_amount_6m,
                               salary_credit_volatility_6m

================================================================================
 INTERNALLY DERIVED FEATURES (NOT USER INPUTS)
================================================================================

  These 8 features are in the model's final feature set but are computed
  automatically by the preprocessing pipeline. Do NOT expose as editable fields.

  flag_has_12m_history      Computed from months_on_book >= 12
  flag_has_cibil_12m        Computed from oldest_tradeline_months >= 12
  flag_cross_sell_offer     Computed from cross_sell_acceptance_rate_12m + mob
  balance_coverage_at_emi   avg_balance_at_emi_due_3m / (emi_amount + 1)
  stress_signal_count       Sum of 7 binary stress indicator conditions
  cibil_monthly_velocity    cibil_change_3m / 3
  emi_cashflow_coverage_gap ratio_emi_to_avg_credit_inflow_3m - emi_burden_ratio
  salary_stress_flag        Derived from salary count and trend columns

================================================================================
 B. BINARY FLAG COLUMNS
================================================================================

  All binary columns accept 0 or 1 only, except where a sentinel of -1 applies
  (see Sentinel Value Convention above).

  Column Name                       | 0 means                  | 1 means
  ----------------------------------|--------------------------|----------------------
  salary_account_flag               | Salary not at this bank  | Salary credited here
  last_payment_on_time_flag         | Payment was late         | Payment was on time
  nach_mandate_active               | No NACH mandate          | NACH mandate active
  salary_credited_last_month_flag   | Not credited last month  | Credited last month
                                    | (-1 if no salary acct)   |
  cibil_trend_flag_deteriorating    | Not deteriorating        | Both 3m & 6m negative
  employer_industry_stress_flag     | Industry stable          | Industry under stress
  employer_distress_news_flag_6m    | No distress news         | Distress news in 6m
  downturn_period_flag              | Normal macro period      | Macro downturn period
